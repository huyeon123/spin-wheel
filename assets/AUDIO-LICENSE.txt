These effects were generated directly from mathematical oscillators, amplitude
envelopes, and deterministic pseudorandom noise. They contain no recordings,
third-party samples, licensed songs, or external sound-font material.

spin.wav is a dry 32 ms individual wooden/mechanical ratchet tick. Schedule one
tick as the wheel crosses each angular step; it is not a looping spin track.
victory.wav is an original 1.24 s C-major arpeggio and resolving chord.
Both files are mono, 22,050 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM. Peak amplitude is limited to
0.33 for the tick and 0.45 for the fanfare, preserving playback headroom.

Rebuild assets and the separate listening preview from the project directory:
    python3 scripts/generate_audio.py
Verify reproducible PCM, format, headroom, DC, and silent endpoints:
    python3 scripts/generate_audio.py --check

The generated listening preview and numerical measurements are written to
build/audio-preview; they are not runtime assets and are excluded from export.
